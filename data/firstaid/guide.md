# First Aid Guide — RoadSoS

## CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation)
1. Check scene is safe — don't put yourself in danger
2. Check response — tap shoulders, shout "Are you okay?"
3. Call emergency services immediately (112 India / 999 Sri Lanka / 191 Thailand)
4. 30 chest compressions — push hard and fast (2 inches deep, 100-120/min)
5. 2 rescue breaths — tilt head, lift chin, pinch nose, breathe in
6. Repeat 30:2 cycle until help arrives or person recovers

## Severe Bleeding
1. Apply firm direct pressure with cloth/clothing
2. Do NOT remove the cloth — add more on top if soaked
3. Elevate the injured limb above heart level if possible
4. Maintain pressure for at least 10 minutes without peeking
5. For limb bleeding — apply tourniquet 2 inches above wound as last resort
6. Keep the person warm and calm — shock is dangerous

## Fractures / Broken Bones
1. Do NOT try to straighten the bone
2. Immobilise the injured area — use whatever is available (stick, magazine, cloth)
3. Tie splint above and below fracture — not too tight
4. Elevate the limb if possible
5. Apply ice pack wrapped in cloth to reduce swelling
6. Monitor for signs of shock (pale, cold, sweating)

## Burns
1. Cool the burn immediately with cool (not cold) running water for 20 minutes
2. Do NOT use ice, butter, toothpaste, or any cream
3. Remove jewellery/clothing near burn — but NOT if stuck to skin
4. Cover loosely with clean cling film or non-fluffy material
5. Do NOT burst blisters
6. Seek medical attention for any burn larger than palm size

## Choking
1. Ask "Are you choking?" — if they can speak, encourage them to cough
2. Give 5 sharp back blows between shoulder blades
3. Give 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich) — stand behind, pull inward and upward
4. Alternate 5 back blows and 5 abdominal thrusts
5. If unconscious, begin CPR immediately

## Shock (After Accident)
1. Lay person down — legs elevated 12 inches unless head/spine/leg injury
2. Keep them warm with blanket or clothing
3. Do NOT give food or drink
4. Loosen tight clothing around neck/chest
5. Talk calmly to keep them conscious
6. Monitor breathing constantly

## Unconscious Person (Breathing)
1. Place in recovery position — on their side
2. Tilt head back, lift chin to open airway
3. Monitor breathing every minute
4. Do NOT leave them alone
5. Call emergency services immediately

## Spinal Injury (Do Not Move)
1. If you suspect neck/spine injury — DO NOT MOVE the person
2. Support head in position found — do not straighten
3. Call emergency immediately
4. Keep airways open by jaw thrust method (not head tilt)
5. Monitor breathing until help arrives
